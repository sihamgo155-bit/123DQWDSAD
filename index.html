DIRECTORATE // DOCUMENT 01

COMMUNITY PROTOCOL

Rules for a safe, fair, and immersive fictional roleplay environment.

01

RESPECT AND CONDUCT

Be respectful to members and staff. Harassment, hate speech, threats, and targeted abuse are not allowed.

02

ROLEPLAY INTEGRITY

Keep actions within agreed roleplay rules. No metagaming, powergaming, or exploiting mechanics to gain unfair advantages.

03

FICTIONAL AGENCY ONLY

CIA COMMAND PORTAL is an independent fictional project. Do not impersonate real officials or represent roleplay activities as real operations.

04

PRIVACY AND SECURITY

Never post personal data, passwords, tokens, or private details belonging to yourself or others.

05

FOLLOW STAFF DECISIONS

Use the appropriate channels to appeal moderation decisions. Do not disrupt operations or retaliate against other players.

06

ACTIVE COMMUNICATION

Notify staff when you cannot meet agreed activity expectations. Respect mission deadlines and team coordination.

07

NO REAL-WORLD MISUSE

No doxxing, surveillance of real people, threats, unlawful access, or instructions to conduct real-world harm under the guise of roleplay.
